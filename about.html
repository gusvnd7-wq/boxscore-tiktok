About BOXSCORE

BOXSCORE is an independent Korean-language sports media brand run by a small in-house team.

Our mission

Sports news is long and scattered. We give fans the one thing worth knowing about each match or record in about 30 seconds, with the numbers checked.

How we work

  • We research the day's most discussed matches, records and players.
  • We verify each claim (scores, dates, quotes, statistics) against published sources before writing.
  • We write in plain, conversational Korean and avoid exaggeration.
  • We publish the same story in several formats: short videos, image carousels, text posts and long-form blog articles.

Our content principles

  • Only verified facts – no rumours, no private-life gossip.
  • Respect for athletes – no insults or harassment.
  • We follow each platform's community guidelines, music-usage and branded-content rules.
