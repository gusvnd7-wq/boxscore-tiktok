BOXSCORE

BOXSCORE is a Korean sports-content brand. This desktop tool lets the account owner publish the brand's own original short sports videos (match results, player stories) to the brand's own TikTok account, @boxscorekr, directly from the production computer.

How it works

  1. The account owner connects the brand account once with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The owner selects a finished MP4 video, enters the title/caption, and chooses the privacy level.
  3. The tool shows the creator's nickname and the privacy options returned by TikTok, then uploads the video with the Content Posting API.
  4. The tool shows the publishing status until the video is posted.

Scopes used

  • user.info.basic – show the connected account's name and avatar.
  • video.publish – post the owner's own videos to the connected account.
  • video.upload – send the video file to TikTok.

The tool is used only by the account owner for the brand's own content. It does not post for third parties, and it does not automate likes, follows or comments.
