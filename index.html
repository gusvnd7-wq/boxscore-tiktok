Olympic & Asian Games sports

Fencing, archery, shooting, swimming, table tennis and more.

How we tell a story

  1. Hook – the result or the surprising number first.
  2. Struggle – what made it difficult.
  3. Turn – the moment it changed.
  4. Result – what it means now.
  5. Question – a question for viewers to answer in the comments.

Every number in our content is checked against a published source before it is used.
