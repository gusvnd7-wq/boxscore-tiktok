Data Deletion

Last updated: October 20, 2026

BOXSCORE's publishing tool is an internal tool used only by the channel owner (the "Operator"). It is not offered to other users.

Data Deletion Instructions

BOXSCORE's publishing tool stores almost nothing about Instagram users. If you commented on one of our posts and received a private reply, we keep only the comment ID, your Instagram username and the time the reply was sent (to avoid messaging you twice). These records are deleted automatically after 30 days.

How to request deletion now

1. Send a Direct Message to @geoul_item (or the account that messaged you) on Instagram with the word "delete", or email QnARobin@gmail.com with your Instagram username.

2. We delete every record linked to your username within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Removing app access

Account owners can remove the app at any time in Instagram: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → Remove. The stored access token is then deleted from the Operator's computer.
