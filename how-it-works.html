How our publishing tool works

BOXSCORE Publisher is the desktop tool our team uses to publish our own finished videos to our own TikTok account (@boxscore.kr). It is used only by the account owner and is not offered to the public.

  1. Connect – the account owner connects the brand account once with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. Choose a video – the owner selects a finished vertical MP4 (under 60 seconds) that our team produced.
  3. Write the caption – the owner reviews and edits the title / caption.
  4. Pick the settings – the tool shows the creator's nickname and the privacy options returned by TikTok; the owner chooses the privacy level and confirms the commercial-content disclosure and Music Usage Confirmation.
  5. Publish – the tool uploads the video with the Content Posting API and shows the publishing status until the video is posted.

Permissions (scopes) used

  • user.info.basic – show the connected account's name and avatar.
  • video.upload – send the video file to TikTok.
  • video.publish – post the owner's own videos to the connected account.

What it does not do

It does not post for third parties, does not collect data about other TikTok users, and does not automate likes, follows or comments. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
