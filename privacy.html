Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Data we access

When the account owner connects the TikTok account with Login Kit, the tool receives an access token, the account's open ID, display name and avatar (user.info.basic). It uses the token only to upload and publish the owner's own videos (video.upload, video.publish).

2. How we use it

Data is used only to show the connected account and to publish the owner's videos. We do not sell, share or disclose it to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage

The access token is stored only on the Operator's own computer. We do not collect data from other TikTok users or viewers.

4. Retention and deletion

The owner can disconnect at any time by removing the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and websites) and by deleting the stored token from the computer. Contact us to request deletion.
